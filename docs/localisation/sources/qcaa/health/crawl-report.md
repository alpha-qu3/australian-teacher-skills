Earlier syllabus versions are not linked from the current Health page and are not registered in this work order.

## Representation and Hash Provenance

Per the coordinator hash-policy ruling (`.superpowers/sdd/2026-08-30-australian-qld-teacher-skills-localisation/progress.md`): raw PDF bytes are preferred, but a documented representation retrieved from the official QCAA URL may be hashed when direct retrieval is blocked. In this environment direct HTTP retrieval of `qcaa.qld.edu.au` returns an HTTP 403 Cloudflare challenge for both HTML and `/downloads/` PDF endpoints (verified with direct curl and webfetch on `2026-09-02`). Firecrawl credentials were exhausted, so each official QCAA URL was retrieved through a reader-rendering route (`https://r.jina.ai/<official QCAA URL>`), which returned the rendered representation of the live official page or PDF.

For every registered source, the recorded `content_hash` is the SHA-256 of the retained rendered-representation payload of the official URL — specifically the bytes of the rendered document content returned under the `Markdown Content:` field (the reader's envelope header lines are excluded). Each payload was inspected before hashing to confirm it is the genuine QCAA document (correct title, publisher, version) and not a challenge page or error page.

## Minimum Subject Matter / Objectives / Assessment Data

The `data/curriculum/qcaa/health.json` contains the minimum required curriculum extraction:

- **Objectives (8):** Recognise and describe information about health-related topics and issues; comprehend and use the Health inquiry model; analyse and interpret information to draw conclusions about health-related topics and issues; critique information to distinguish determinants that influence health status; investigate and synthesise information to develop action strategies; evaluate and reflect on implemented action strategies to justify recommendations that mediate, advocate and enable health promotion; organise information for particular purposes; make decisions about and use mode-appropriate features, language and conventions for particular purposes and contexts.
- **Subject matter (4 units):** Unit 1 Resilience as a personal health resource; Unit 2 Peers and family as resources for healthy living (Elective topic 1: Alcohol; Elective topic 2: Body image); Unit 3 Community as a resource for healthy living (Elective topic 1: Homelessness; Elective topic 2: Transport safety; Elective topic 3: Anxiety); Unit 4 Respectful relationships in the post-schooling transition. Each unit has a title and a short description; Units 1 and 2 are preparatory, Units 3 and 4 are studied as a pair.
- **Assessment model:** IA1 Action research (25%, Unit 3); IA2 Examination — extended response (25%, Unit 3); IA3 Investigation (25%, Unit 4); EA Examination — extended response (25%, developed and marked by QCAA, relates to Unit 4).
- **Source locators:** All 14 official URLs are recorded in the source register and the canonical URLs for the syllabus, key changes, inquiry model resource, QA confirmation, sample instruments and 2025 past papers are recorded in `source_locators` in the curriculum JSON.
