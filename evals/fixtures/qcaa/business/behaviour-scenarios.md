# Behaviour Scenarios for QCAA Business

## Lesson Plan Creation

**Scenario:** A teacher develops a 90-minute lesson plan for Year 11 Business students focusing on financial statements analysis (Unit 3, Topic 2). The lesson includes: introducing the concepts of liquidity, profitability, and solvency ratios; analysing actual financial statements from Australian companies; having students calculate and interpret key financial ratios; and discussing how these ratios inform business decision-making. The lesson aligns with syllabus objectives 1 (recall), 2 (interpret), 3 (analyse), and 4 (evaluate) by having students work with real business data to assess company financial health.

## Lesson Differentiation

**Scenario:** A teacher differentiates a Unit 2, Topic 3 lesson on marketing strategies to support diverse learners including EAL/D students and students requiring additional processing time. For the core activity where students develop marketing mixes for different target markets, the teacher provides: simplified case studies with visual aids for EAL/D students; extended timelines and optional scaffoling worksheets for students needing additional processing time; advanced extension challenges for gifted students analysing digital marketing campaigns; and alternative assessment options allowing students to demonstrate understanding through oral presentations instead of written reports. The differentiation maintains alignment with syllabus objectives 2 (interpret), 4 (evaluate), and 5 (communicate) while providing multiple means of engagement, representation, and expression.

## Check for Understanding

**Scenario:** During a Unit 4 lesson on business planning, a teacher uses a combination of entrance tickets and think-pair-share activities to check student understanding of the business planning process. Students are given a scenario involving a new startup business and must: (1) identify the key components of a business plan, (2) explain the purpose of each component, and (3) discuss how these components work together to create a viable business plan. Entrance tickets prompt students to list three essential components of a business plan. Think-pair-share activities involve students discussing their responses with a partner before sharing with the class. This checks understanding of syllabus objective 4 (evaluate) specifically applied to business planning processes.

## Lesson Preparation

**Scenario:** A teacher prepares to introduce the business functions concept (Unit 1, Topic 2) by reviewing syllabus requirements and gathering resources. Preparation includes: studying the business functions diagram showing interconnected areas (operations, marketing, finance, human resources); identifying key principles affecting all business functions (ethics, sustainability, legal compliance); preparing examples of financial statements (income statement, balance sheet, cash flow statement); and designing a hands-on activity where students map local businesses to business function categories. The teacher verifies that the lesson addresses syllabus objective 1 (recall) by ensuring students can identify the four main business functions and explain their interrelationships.
