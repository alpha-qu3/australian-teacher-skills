# HASS — Humanities and Social Studies differentiation pedagogy

Loaded by `australian-lesson-differentiation` when the subject is **hass**.

## Identify the source lesson and curriculum

**Detect QCAA use.** Before anything else, check whether the teacher is using QCAA Queensland curriculum. Look for signals anywhere in the conversation: explicit name ("QCAA", "Queensland Curriculum and Assessment Authority"), QCAA-specific terminology, or an uploaded QCAA syllabus document. If confirmed, treat as **QCAA-confirmed** throughout — use QCAA discourse language and structures in the teacher plan.

Then detect which scenario applies:

**Scenario A — Source lesson exists in the prior conversation**
If a lesson was produced or discussed earlier in this conversation, use it directly. Do NOT re-ask the teacher to share it.

**Scenario B — Teacher uploads a source lesson**
Read the file first. Confirm: year level, subject, standard, learning objective, lesson structure. If the file is unreadable on first attempt, surface the error clearly and ask the teacher to re-share. Do NOT silently fabricate a lesson.

**Scenario B2 — Teacher links a source lesson by URL**
Fetch the URL and read its content first. Then confirm year level, subject, standard, learning objective, and lesson structure exactly as in Scenario B. If the fetch fails or returns unusable content, surface the error clearly and ask the teacher to paste the lesson text or upload the file. Do NOT silently fabricate a lesson.

**Fetching the lesson completes Step 1 only — it does NOT replace curriculum grounding.** Continue to Step 2 — Ground in standards. Skipping Step 2 after a URL fetch is the same critical failure as skipping it for an uploaded lesson.

**Scenario C — No source lesson present**
Ask before proceeding:
> "Happy to differentiate. Do you have a specific lesson in mind? You can paste it, share a file, or tell me the year level + topic + standard and I'll work from that."
